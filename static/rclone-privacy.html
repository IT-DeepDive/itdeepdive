rclone-gdrive – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

rclone-gdrive is a personal, single-user Google OAuth client operated by the owner of itdeepdive.com (contact: rugginic@gmail.com). It is used only to let rclone mount the owner's own Google Drive on the owner's own computer.

Data accessed

The app requests access to Google Drive files (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive) solely to read and write the authorizing user's files on their own device.

Data use and sharing

  • Data flows only between Google Drive and the user's own machine.
  • No data is collected, stored on any server, sold, or shared with third parties.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
